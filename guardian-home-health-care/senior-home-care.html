Senior home care

Familiar routines.
A thoughtful presence.

Non-medical senior care begins with the individual: their home, their preferences and the everyday moments that matter.

Home is personal. Care should be, too.

An older adult’s home holds routines, relationships and a sense of independence. Guardians’ approach starts by respecting that familiarity and listening to the person who lives there. We believe additional support should protect dignity and help a person continue to feel valued.

What we can discuss

  • Your loved one’s preferred daily routine
  • The general kind of everyday support you are seeking
  • Companionship and the importance of human connection
  • The days and hours that would be helpful to your family

Before we speak

A few helpful answers.

What kind of care does Guardians provide?

Guardians focuses on non-medical home care: compassionate companionship, everyday support and reassurance for older adults and their families. Specific tasks, suitability, availability and any applicable authorization requirements are discussed before a care arrangement begins. This is not a medical treatment service.

Can we discuss daytime or overnight support?

Yes. We provide daytime and overnight care for as many hours as your loved one needs, upon arrangement, and round-the-clock care can be arranged. Share the days, hours or shifts your family has in mind, and we will plan the schedule together.

How is pricing determined?

Care is discussed on an hourly basis. Cost varies with the individual arrangement, number of hours and daytime or overnight schedule. Contact us for a conversation about your needs and a personalized discussion of costs.
