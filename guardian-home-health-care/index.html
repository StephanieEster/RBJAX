Questions, answered

Making room for clarity.

Every family’s circumstances are different. Here are a few useful things to know before we speak.

Ask Ana a question
Can I book care immediately through the website?

Guardian is a newly established company preparing its licensing application. This website welcomes inquiries; a form submission does not book care. Any arrangement is subject to required authorizations, an individual review and confirmed availability.

What kind of care does Guardian provide?

Guardian focuses on non-medical home care: compassionate companionship, everyday support and reassurance for older adults and their families. Specific tasks, suitability, availability and any applicable authorization requirements are discussed before a care arrangement begins. This is not a medical treatment service.

Where do you offer home care?

Our service area focuses on Lake Mary (32746), Maitland, Sanford, Heathrow and Winter Park, Florida. Contact Ana to confirm whether your home is within the service area.

Can we discuss daytime or overnight support?

Yes. Share the days, hours or overnight shifts your family has in mind. Care arrangements depend on individual needs, availability and applicable authorization requirements; round-the-clock coverage is not guaranteed.

Who will I speak with about care?

You’ll start with Ana Roberts, the founder. She takes time to understand your family’s priorities and discuss the next steps personally.

Do you provide medical treatment or integrative medicine?

The website describes non-medical home care. It does not offer diagnoses, laboratory testing, medication changes, skilled nursing or integrative medical treatment. Those needs should be discussed with your licensed medical provider.

How is pricing determined?

Care is discussed on an hourly basis. Cost varies with the individual arrangement, number of hours and daytime or overnight schedule. Contact Ana for a conversation about your needs and a personalized discussion of costs.

What information should I share through the form?

Please share your name, phone number, email and general care interest. Do not submit medical records, diagnoses, medication information or other sensitive health details through this website. Specific care needs can be discussed directly through an appropriate channel.
