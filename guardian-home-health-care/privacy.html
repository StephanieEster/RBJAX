Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026.

This policy explains how Guardians Home Health Care handles information provided through this website and related care inquiries.

Information you choose to provide

The inquiry forms collect your name, telephone number, email address, care interest and consent. The contact form also allows an optional city and message. Please do not provide medical records, diagnoses, medication details, insurance information, financial account information or other sensitive health information through these forms.

How inquiry information is used

Information is used to respond to your inquiry, discuss care interests and scheduling, and communicate about requested support. A submitted inquiry is sent by email to the company. The forms do not book or confirm care.

Calls, email and text messages

When you check the consent box, you agree to receive calls, text messages and emails from Guardians Home Health Care about your request. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies with the conversation and your request. Message and data rates may apply. A form submission does not automatically send a text message.

Reply STOP to a text message to opt out of further texts. Reply HELP for help, or call (321) 977-3169 or email guardianshomehealthllc@gmail.com. You may also use those contact details to request that we stop calls or emails. A limited confirmation of a text opt-out may be sent where applicable.

Protection of consent information

Mobile information, text-message opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be disclosed to providers as necessary to support communications, security or website delivery, or when required by law. Providers must not use consent data for their own marketing.

Website operation

The site uses session storage in your browser to remember whether a care inquiry popup has been shown or you have begun an inquiry. This setting is limited to the browsing session. There are no advertising trackers or analytics scripts included in this website package.

Fonts and all other website files are served directly from our own hosting; no third-party font or content service is contacted when you browse. The hosting service may record technical information such as request times, browser details and IP addresses. The form endpoint uses a hashed network identifier and submission timestamp in temporary server storage to reduce repeated submissions.

Security and retention

The website is intended to use HTTPS after deployment. Form information is handled through the server and sent to the company’s email inbox. No website transmission or email channel can be guaranteed completely secure. Information should be retained only for responding to inquiries, managing relevant communications and meeting applicable obligations; contact the company with questions about an existing inquiry.

Your choices

Contact us to ask about information you submitted, request correction or deletion where applicable, or withdraw communication consent. Some records may need to be retained to meet applicable obligations or record a communication opt-out.

Children

This website is intended for adults discussing care. Do not submit personal information about a child through the inquiry forms.
