Professional Kitchen Backsplash Done Right

The backsplash is right at eye level, so small mistakes are easy to see. Uneven spacing, crooked lines or rough cuts around outlets and windows can ruin an otherwise beautiful kitchen. Our fine-finish approach is what our clients notice first.

Whether you want classic white subway tile, a herringbone or chevron pattern, glass mosaic or a sleek large-format slab look, we plan the layout so the pattern is centered and balanced across your counters.

What we install

  • Subway tile in straight, offset or vertical stack
  • Herringbone and chevron patterns
  • Glass, stone and ceramic mosaics
  • Large-format and slab-look backsplash
  • Clean cuts around outlets, windows and hoods
  • Metal or tile edge trims for a finished look
  • Accent walls and coffee-bar backsplashes
  • Removal of old backsplash when needed

FAQ

Kitchen Backsplash Questions

How long does a backsplash installation take?

Most kitchen backsplashes are completed quickly — often in one to two days depending on size and pattern. We confirm the schedule during your free estimate.

Do I need to buy the tile before the estimate?

No. We can visit first, measure your kitchen and help you understand how much tile you need and which patterns work best for your space.

Can you remove my old backsplash?

Yes. We can remove the existing backsplash, repair the wall and prepare it for new tile.
