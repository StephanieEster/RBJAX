FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the estimate really free?

Yes — 100% free, with no obligation, anywhere in our New Hampshire service area. Many contractors charge just to come out and quote; we don't.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Manchester and cities within about 50 miles in New Hampshire, including Nashua, Concord, Bedford, Londonderry, Derry, Merrimack, Salem, Hudson and Exeter. We do not work in Massachusetts or Maine.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Every job includes a 1-year warranty covering workmanship and product.

What is the best way to contact you?

Text us at (978) 331-6200 or fill out the estimate form. Texting is the fastest way to share photos of your space and schedule your free estimate.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Zelle, cash, check and Venmo.

What types of tile do you install?

Porcelain, ceramic, large-format, mosaic, glass, marble-look and natural-stone-look tile for showers, bathrooms, floors, backsplashes and fireplaces.
