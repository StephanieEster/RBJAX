Website and communication policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

This policy explains how RB Jax Cleaning Services LLC, doing business as RB Jax Cleaning Services, collects, uses and protects information submitted through this website or shared during communications with the company.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, property type, service interests, location details, preferred contact method and information you choose to include in a request. Technical information such as IP address, browser type and basic server logs may also be processed for security and website operation.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to requests, arrange property walkthroughs, prepare or discuss service estimates, schedule cleaning services, provide customer support, maintain records and protect the website against spam or abuse.

SMS / Text Messaging

When you opt in to receive text messages from RB Jax Cleaning Services LLC, we collect your mobile phone number and your consent so we can send the messages you agreed to receive.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver our messages (such as our SMS platform), who are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP or contact us at rbcleaningfl@gmail.com or (973) 849-7559.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share mobile opt-in information or text message consent with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the website, deliver communications, process requested services, comply with law or protect legal rights.

Data security and retention

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect personal information. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, business records, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information by contacting us. Certain information may be retained when required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

External links

This website may link to social media platforms. Their privacy practices are controlled by those platforms, and you should review their policies before sharing information with them.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when practices or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy may be sent to rbcleaningfl@gmail.com or discussed by calling +1 (727) 877-3413.
