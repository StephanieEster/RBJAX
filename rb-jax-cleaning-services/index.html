Questions before your visit

Helpful answers, before we meet.

What is the difference between a deep clean and regular cleaning?

Deep cleaning is the more detailed first service used to reset the property. Regular cleaning maintains the space afterward. The exact scope is confirmed during the in-person walkthrough.

Do you provide prices over the phone?

No. Every property is different, so we visit the space before providing a price. This makes the estimate clearer and avoids surprises about the scope.

Can I schedule recurring cleaning?

Yes. Weekly, biweekly and monthly cleaning options are available and can be planned around the needs of the property.

Is exterior window cleaning included?

Exterior window cleaning is offered separately. Ask about it during your walkthrough so it can be included in the scope.

What areas do you serve?

The company serves Jacksonville and nearby communities within an approximate 30-mile radius. Contact us with your location to confirm availability.

What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment options include Zelle, cash, check and Venmo.
