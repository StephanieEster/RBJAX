Good to know

Questions we hear before a project.

How long will my painting project take?

Timing depends on the size of the space, the condition of the surfaces, and the work involved. We can discuss a realistic schedule after seeing your project.

Do I need to move my furniture before you arrive?

Please move small personal items before the work begins. We can help move larger furniture and protect it as part of the project preparation; we will confirm the details during your estimate.

Will the paint smell linger?

Paint odor depends on the product and ventilation in the space. We can discuss product options and ways to air out the area when planning the project.

Do you provide an estimate?

Yes. Fernando will discuss your needs, arrange a visit when appropriate, and send a written estimate describing the proposed work, usually the same day or the following day after the visit.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Greater Boston and surrounding communities, extending west toward and beyond Worcester for projects within our service range. Text or call with your location so we can confirm availability.
