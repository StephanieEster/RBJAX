Our story

A business built one project at a time.

Before founding FG Professional Services in 2022, Fernando spent years in painting, working closely with clients and learning what a well-run project should feel like.

He started his own company to bring that experience directly to homeowners. Today, with 12 years of experience behind him, Fernando remains involved in the work alongside a consistent three-person crew.

We believe careful preparation, respect for your belongings, and attention to finish details make the difference you notice every day.
