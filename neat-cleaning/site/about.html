Meet Daiane

Neat Cleaning is an owner-led cleaning business in Natick, Massachusetts, founded in 2021, providing regular, deep, Airbnb, move-in and move-out, and commercial cleaning within 25 miles.

Neat Cleaning is led by Daiane Ventura de Oliveira Hotis. She takes care of the cleaning herself, with additional help arranged as demand requires. The person who hears how you like your home cared for is the person responsible for doing it.

Daiane responds to text messages, and her daughter helps answer calls. You can discuss your home, your priorities and the service you need before an appointment is confirmed.

Regular and deep cleaning are the primary focus. Neat also offers Airbnb turnovers, move-in and move-out cleaning and commercial cleaning for local workspaces.
