Biweekly cleaning

Why so many families choose every two weeks

For a busy household in a 3-bedroom, 2–3 bathroom home, biweekly cleaning hits the sweet spot: the house stays clean, the routine stays simple, and your weekends stay yours.

  • A consistently clean home

    Kitchens and bathrooms never get far from "just cleaned."

  • Easier upkeep

    Two weeks is short enough that buildup never gets a head start.

  • A predictable routine

    Same rhythm, every other week. One less thing to plan.

  • Weekends back

    Less Saturday scrubbing, more time for the people you live with.

  • Professional maintenance

    Experienced hands keep surfaces and fixtures in good shape.

  • Flexible when needed

    Two teams mean we can often rearrange a visit when your week changes.
